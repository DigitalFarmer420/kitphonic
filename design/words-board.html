Design notes — Next levels

    2 · word families

  1. 14
    Kit gives the instruction. The 🔊 (64 px) replays it.
  2. 15
    The picture card stays “?” until a real word is made, then the picture pops in.
  3. 16
    The rime tile “at” is fixed: honey-tinted with a pressed-in look, not draggable. The empty slot pulses coral.
  4. 17
    Onset tiles are 68×88 and reusable. A drag or a tap-to-place (tap the tile, then the slot) both work, since some 4-year-olds can’t drag yet.
  5. 18
    Ghost hand drags “m” along the dashed arc on the first try and after 5 s idle.
  6. 19
    The words made are 64 px chips that read the word on tap. All 4 earn the family ⭐.

    7. 3 · digraphs

  7. 20
    A digraph is one joined sky-blue tile with an underline bar. It’s the same colour for every digraph, everywhere in the app.
  8. 21
    The slider has one stop per sound, not per letter: “ship” is 4 letters but 3 stops. Holding the knob on sh plays a continuous “shhh”.
  9. 22
    Picture check after blending uses minimal pairs, as in config.js: sheep (vowel) and chip (sh vs ch). The child has to blend, not guess from the first sound.
  10. 23
    Teaching: the first time, s + h fuse into one tile (see mini state). Kit says “two letters, one sound”. Never sound it out as /s/-/h/.

    11. 4 · star word

  11. 24
    The sentence strip has 76 px word chips. The current word gets a coral outline, upcoming ones are faded, and done ones turn sage.
  12. 25
    The star word is gold with a ⭐. A tap plays the word, with no slider because it isn’t decodable yet. Star words start at L2, max 2 per level.
  13. 26
    Decodable words use the usual tiles and slider. Sounds already passed turn sage.
  14. 27
    “Read it all” (76 px) unlocks after the last word. Kit reads the sentence karaoke-style and the picture is revealed.
