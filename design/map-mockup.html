Notes

  1. Top: home, star count, grown-up gear (press-and-hold gate). Nothing else.
  2. Current stop: big honey circle with a glow and Kit sitting next to it. Tapping it or ▶ starts today's 5-minute lesson.
  3. Stops ahead are locked (sand color, no padlock icon, so it doesn't feel scary). Each stop's symbol shows what's inside (a, s, mat, sh, 📖), not a number. Labels are for parents.
  4. One huge ▶ (200×72) in thumb reach always starts "the next right thing", including the review of shaky sounds (the 1/3/7/14/30-day review). The child never chooses a level.
  5. Level-up: the stop flies into a big star badge with confetti and a chime, and Kit cheers in his own voice ("You did it!"). Plays for about 3 seconds, can't be skipped for the first 1.5 s.
  6. The 3 words they mastered come back as tappable chips that read each word aloud, so they hear what they've learned.
  7. One ➜ (220×80) back to the map, where the path draws forward to the next stop. Star words stay inside each stop, at most 2 per level, so they don't get their own stop.

Stop list (1.0 + later): letters → sounds → blend L1–L5 → word families → sh/ch/st → star sentences → little stories.
