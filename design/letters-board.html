Design notes — Letters

    1a · find the letter

  1. 1
    Home and pause are 64 px. The dots show where the child is in a ~5-min session and contain no numbers.
  2. 2
    Every instruction is spoken. The 🔊 button (64 px) replays Kit. The captions are 12 px grey and only for parents.
  3. 3
    The big ear (170 px) replays only the sound /s/, never the letter name.
  4. 4
    3 tiles at 100×150 in Andika 100 px, lowercase. Early distractors are visually distinct (m, t). b/d/p/q never appear together before L3.
  5. 5
    Ghost hand: after 5 s idle it taps the ear, then hovers the tiles. It also plays once on the first item.
  6. 6
    Wrong tap: the tile says its own sound and wobbles gently. No red, ✕ or buzzer. After 2 misses the right tile glows softly.
  7. 7
    Right tap: sage, star and chime, then auto-advance after 1.2 s.

    8. 1b · letter intro

  8. 8
    A 250 px letter card. Tapping anywhere plays the sound.
  9. 9
    The picture starts with that sound, and the first letter is shown in coral. A tap plays “sss… sun”.
  10. 10
    One big ➜ (76 px) to go on, and 🔁 to hear it again.

    11. 1c · trace

  11. 11
    A green start dot numbered 1, with honey arrows at each turn and a hollow end dot.
  12. 12
    A wide 58 px lane gives a forgiving finger tolerance. The coral ink follows the finger, and the ghost hand demos the stroke first.
  13. 13
    Trace 3 times to earn 3 stars. Undo (64 px) clears the last stroke.
